BIRYANI



Ingredients


For Marinade
  • 500g chicken
  • 3/4 cup thick yogurt
  • 1½ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon red chili powder
  • 1/4 tablespoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon biryani masala
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons mint leaves
  • Salt to taste

For Rice
  • 1½cups aged basmati rice
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cloves, 4 green cardamoms, 1 cinnamon stick , 1 star anise
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • 1½tablespoons salt
For Layering
  • 2-3 tablespoons fried onions
  • 2 tablespoons saffron mil
  • 1 tablespoon ghee
  • Fresh mint and coriander leaves


Preparation Steps

  1. Marinate the Chicken:-
    Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, lemon juice, and herbs. Marinate for at least 2 hours (overnight for best results).
  2. Cook the Rice :-
    Boil water with spices, oil, and salt. Add soaked basmati rice and cook until 70–80% done. Drain and keep aside.
  3. Layer and Cook :-
    In a heavy-bottomed pot, add marinated chicken as the bottom layer. Add half-cooked rice on top. Sprinkle fried onions, saffron milk, ghee, and herbs. Cover tightly and seal with dough or a tight lid. Cook on low heat for 25–30 minutes (dum method) for flavors to infuse evenly
  4. Serving :-
    Fluff the biryani gently with a fork before serving. Pair with raita, boiled eggs, or salad.
