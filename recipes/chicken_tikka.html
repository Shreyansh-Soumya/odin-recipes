CHICKEN TIKKA




Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken (breast or thighs, cut into chunks)
  • cup thick yogurt (Greek or hung curd)
  • 1½ tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1–2 teaspoons Kashmiri red chili powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon mustard oil (or any oil)
  • 1 tablespoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • Salt to taste
  • Bell peppers and onion cubes (optional, for skewering and flavor)

Preparation Steps


  1. Marinate the Chicken :-
    In a bowl, combine yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, chili powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, kasuri methi, salt, and mustard oil into a smooth mixture. Add chicken pieces and coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours (or up to 8 hours for best flavor).
  2. Cook the Chicken :-
    Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) or prepare a grill on medium heat. Thread chicken pieces (and vegetables if using) onto skewers. Grill or bake for 20–25 minutes, turning occasionally and brushing with butter or oil until slightly charred and cooked through. For a smoky flavor, briefly expose the cooked chicken to coal smoke inside a covered bowl for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Serve :-
    Garnish with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon juice. Serve hot with mint chutney, onion slices, and naan or paratha.
